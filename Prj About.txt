
Banking Customer Churn Prediction and Retention Recommendation System

I worked on a project that delivers an end-to-end churn prediction and retention recommendation system for a retail bank, transforming raw customer data into actionable business decisions. 
The workflow includes structured data cleaning and feature engineering, training and evaluating multiple models: 
1. Logistic Regression – used as a baseline model to establish a simple, interpretable benchmark for churn prediction. 
2. XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting) – used as the final model due to its superior performance in capturing non-linear patterns. 
XGBoost was selected as the final model, and ranking customers by both churn risk and estimated financial impact. 
Customers are segmented into meaningful groups, and model outputs are translated into targeted retention actions. 
A production-style inference pipeline applies the same preprocessing and model logic to new customers, demonstrating how the system can be operationalized at scale.
